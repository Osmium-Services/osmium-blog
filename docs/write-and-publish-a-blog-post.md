Blog posts are written in **Osmium Blog** and published as ordinary pages on your site.

## Write a post

1. Open **Osmium Blog** and press **New Post**.
2. Enter the **Title** and write the **Post**.
3. Add pictures. **Choose image** fills an image slot, and the post editor has an **Add image** button for pictures inside the text. Give each picture a **Description (alt text)**, which describes it for screen readers and search engines.
4. Choose the **Author**.
5. Press **Save**.

A new post stays a draft until you tick **Published**.

## Publish or schedule

Tick **Published** and save to put the post live.

To schedule it, tick **Published** and choose a future **Publish date** and time. The post goes live then, with nothing else to do. If you leave the date empty, it is set when the post is first published.

## Search and sharing

Under **Search and sharing**:

- **Address** is the post's web address. If you change it later, the old address redirects to the new one.
- **Link text** is the wording of the post's link where posts are listed, such as on the home page. Leave it blank for "Read more".
- **Meta description** is the text search engines may show under the title.
- **Excerpt** is a short summary for the blog index.

## Link to a page

In the post editor, **Link to a page** lets you pick one of your pages to link to instead of typing the address.
