## Delete a post

Use **Delete** on a post's row. You are asked for an optional **Redirect to** address.

- Leave it empty and visitors get a 404. This is faster for removing the page from search results.
- Enter a page and visitors, and search ranking, are sent there instead.

The post is not lost. You can restore it from **Deleted Posts** on the same page.

## Blog settings

Press **Settings** on the Osmium Blog page.

- **Section address** is the page the posts are listed on, and the start of every post's address. The default is `blog`. A site might use `guides` instead. Your site's design needs matching pages for any new section name, so ask your developer before changing it.
- **Default author** is who a new post is credited to. Choose **Whoever is writing the post** or a named person. You can still change the author on each post.
